Omega Duel Log

A tool for analyzing the metagame and your win rate conditional on many factors (e.g. going 1st vs going 2nd, by card in opening hand, etc.). The necessary data is your deck and replay information from YGO Omega; details below.

Load your Omega folder

or drag the folder onto this box
  1. Mac: in Finder, open Applications, right-click YGO Omega → Show Package Contents → Contents, then drag the Files folder here. (The Choose folder dialog can't see inside apps; dragging is easiest.)
  2. Windows: choose the Files folder inside your YGO Omega install (it holds Replays and Player.data).
  3. Only replays (Replays/Client/*.bytes), your decklists (Player.data) and their backups are read.
  4. Several players: drop a folder of contributed data packs (the folder from Google Drive, unzipped once). Each player's packs are combined and a Player filter appears.

Contribute your games

Share your YGO Omega replays and decklists with this project. Only replays and decklists are packed; nothing else in your Omega folder is read. Your opponents' account numbers are replaced with anonymous codes before anything leaves this page.
